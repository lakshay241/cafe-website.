How we brew

Four steps, every morning

Step 1

Roast

Beans from a Chikmagalur estate are roasted dark twice a week, in small batches.

Step 2

Blend

We grind them fine with chicory, which gives filter coffee its body and slight bitterness.

Step 3

Drip

Hot water drips through the brass filter for about 20 minutes to make a thick decoction.

Step 4

Pour

Decoction meets boiled milk and sugar, then gets poured from a height until it froths.
