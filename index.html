The Process

Our Coffee Journey

From bean to cup — a tradition of taste.

We started in 2015 with one brass filter and a small counter. Every morning we still grind our beans with 20% chicory and drip the decoction slowly, the way it is done in homes across Karnataka and Tamil Nadu.

  1. 1. Source

    Premium beans from Chikmagalur estates.

  2. 2. Roast

    Dark roasted in small batches.

  3. 3. Filter

    Slow drip for rich flavour and aroma.

  4. 4. Serve

    With hot milk and a touch of love.
